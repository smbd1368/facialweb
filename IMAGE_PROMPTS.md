# FaceShiraz visual direction

The production hero was generated with the built-in image generation tool and saved at `public/images/faceshiraz-hero.png`.

## Hero / editorial system prompt

High-end photorealistic beauty editorial for a luxury facial skincare studio in Shiraz; elegant Iranian woman with healthy natural dewy skin, serene expression, contemporary spa with travertine, ivory linen and subtle arches; cinematic wide framing with quiet negative space; diffused morning light; warm ivory, sand, blush nude and champagne accents; realistic texture; no logos, text, watermark, medical procedures, clutter or exaggerated retouching.

## Additional facial campaign assets

Six additional images were generated with the same warm ivory editorial direction:

- `facial-consultation.png` — specialist consulting with an Iranian client.
- `facial-cleansing.png` — gentle cleansing with cotton pads.
- `facial-steam.png` — safe steam preparation in a calm treatment room.
- `facial-mask.png` — creamy hydrating mask application.
- `facial-massage.png` — relaxing professional facial massage.
- `skincare-products.png` — unbranded serum, cream, towels and gua sha still life.

All prompts required photorealistic natural skin, diffused morning light, warm ivory and muted blush colors, a clean contemporary spa, and excluded text, logos, watermarks, invasive procedures, clutter and exaggerated retouching.

## Homepage scroll-story images
